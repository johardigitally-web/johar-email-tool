How this flow works, and what stops it

Nobody gets two emails. Somebody can only be in one flow at a time, and no email goes out within a day of any other email to the same person, campaign or flow. So an abandoned cart email and a late checkout reminder cannot both land.

Editing. Edit on any email changes what it says and how long it waits, and takes effect on the next run. Pausing the flow leaves everybody exactly where they are.

Where the orders come from. The Results screen matches everyone who was emailed against everyone who then bought, in the webshop and in the showroom, and credits the last message they received.

How this differs from a campaign. A campaign goes when you press send. This goes on its own, so it has its own brakes: a separate switch (FLOWS_ENABLED), a start date it can never reach back past (FLOWS_FROM), its own daily cap, and it stops the moment somebody orders, unsubscribes or bounces.
