How this works

Every email is a stack of blocks. A header, a strip, text, products, a button, and so on. A template is the order you put them in plus the words you type as a starting point. The logo and the footer are always there and cannot be removed, because the footer carries the lines the law requires.

Nothing here changes an email you already made. A campaign bakes its design in when you save it. Editing or deleting a template only changes what the next campaign starts from.

Flows keep their own design. Templates are for campaigns.
